The images are Wikimedia Commons community-contributed photographs, not
commissioned or exclusive Lativera assets. Review the source page and license
terms again before substantially modifying or redistributing an image.
The Manaus and Belém local files use 1280-pixel Wikimedia Commons thumbnails;
the Alter do Chão image is served at the source's 1200-pixel size. Attribution
and share-alike terms are listed for all three files above.

Flight route pages use the actual cities as destination imagery. These photographs
depict the city or landmark named, not the airports or the specific flight path.
The hero's generic airliner photograph is not presented as an aircraft operating
on any route. The Brasília photograph depicts the Eixo Monumental from the TV
Tower, not the airport.
